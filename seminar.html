ACM CUI Student Seminar

One emerging researcher. One fresh paper. One open conversation.

A new online series from the student community of ACM CUI, bringing early-career research on speech, chatbots, and conversational AI to everyone around the world. The pilot season launches in February 2027.

Present or attend Contact the organizers for questions
01

How it works

Each session is a relaxed, hour-long conversation with one presenter about one recent paper, with plenty of time for the questions that never make it into the paper.

Format

1 hour · 1 presenter

A talk on one recent paper, followed by Q&A and open discussion.

Who presents

Emerging researchers

PhD students, master's researchers, and postdocs, on work from the last two years.

Who attends

Everyone, online

Open to anyone interested in conversational user interfaces, anywhere in the world.

When

From Feb 2027

The pilot season starts in February 2027. Times are planned around sign-ups' availability.
