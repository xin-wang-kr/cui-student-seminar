Present. Listen. Connect.

Conversational AI is moving fast, and some of its most exciting ideas are coming from researchers at the start of their careers. The CUI Student Seminar is a new online series that brings that work to you, open to everyone around the world.

One emerging researcher. One fresh paper. One open conversation.

PhD students · master's researchers · postdocs

Want to present?

Get your work in front of peers worldwide, practice your talk in a friendly room, and walk away with fresh ideas for your next paper, deadline, or defense.

Everyone, anywhere

Want to listen?

Hear new research straight from the people who did it, ask about the choices and dead ends that never make it into the paper, and meet others working on the same problems you are.
